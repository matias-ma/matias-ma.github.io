Matias Andia

Mathematics · Princeton University

I am in the final year of my undergraduate studies in mathematics at Princeton. My interests are in numerical analysis, particularly tensor methods and high-dimensional function approximation, with applications to cryo-electron microscopy. I am also interested in Gaussian elimination with complete pivoting and related matrix-growth questions.

I retain a broad interest in particle physics, though I spend less time on it these days than I would like.

Please feel free to reach out if you’d like to discuss any of these topics.

Preprints

A Hadamard matrix with growth factor greater than its order

Matias Andia · 2026

A completely pivoted Hadamard matrix of order 4096 with growth factor 4608, giving a counterexample to the conjecture that the growth factor equals the order. The construction extends to every power-of-two order at least 4096.

A completely pivoted Hadamard matrix with a non-final pivot equal to n

Matias Andia · 2026

An order-64 construction whose sixtieth pivot equals 64, disproving three conjectures about non-final pivots. The paper also determines the possible pairs of fifth and sixth pivot magnitudes for Hadamard matrices under complete pivoting.

A functional tensor train by recursive Gaussian elimination

Joint work with Marc Aurèle Gilles

Recursive Gaussian elimination builds a functional tensor train without evaluating a full tensor-product grid. The poster presents convergence guarantees under analyticity assumptions and numerical examples.

Presented at the 11th International Conference on Curves and Surfaces, 2026.

Tensor density estimation in cryo-EM

Joint work with Marc Aurèle Gilles

Tensor-train density estimation for conformational heterogeneity, with initial comparisons against kernel density estimation on synthetic cryo-EM data and a 10-dimensional Ginzburg–Landau distribution.

Presented at the SIAM New York–New Jersey–Pennsylvania Section Conference, 2025.
